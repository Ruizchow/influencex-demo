## Short English post

I've published a self-hostable creator marketing workspace based on the MIT-licensed
InfluenceX project: creator discovery, campaign tracking, content planning, and outreach
review in one place.

This edition adds a Render deployment path, a local setup helper, and English/Chinese
docs. You can explore the local sample workspace without API keys; real AI, discovery,
email, and publishing need their respective providers.

Code: https://github.com/Ruizchow/influencex-demo
Live homepage: https://influencex-kikm.onrender.com (workspace access is invite-only)
